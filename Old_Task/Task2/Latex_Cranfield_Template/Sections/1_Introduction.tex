\section{Introduction}

Group Chenonceau was formed to undertake the "Applications of Computational Engineering Design Optimisation" project. The group consists of four members from the MSc in Computational Software Techniques in Engineering.

An initial meeting was held to discuss the individual strengths and development opportunities identified in Task 1. This report synthesises those findings into a cohesive team structure, ensuring that the Belbin team roles \cite{hoarauTask1} are distributed to maximise efficiency. Additionally, this report details the digital infrastructure the team will use to collaborate remotely between ESTIA (France) and Cranfield (UK).
